04

About

Reporters and analysts do the same thing: read the file until it gives something up.

I started in newsrooms in Bogotá, went to Amsterdam to learn data journalism with OneWorld, and took an MA in Data Journalism at Tilburg University on a full merit scholarship. In 2017 I worked the Colombian side of the Paradise Papers alongside ICIJ, Connectas and El Espectador.

In 2018 I co-founded Cuestión Pública and spent seven years building it — editorial strategy, the systems that produced the reporting, and the social desk that carried it to 143,000 people. Its investigations went on to win the Premio Rey de España, the Premio Simón Bolívar, the Premio CPB and the SIP award.

Now I work where the two halves meet. Due diligence for people who need to know who is actually behind a company. Market research in two languages. And AI pipelines built so that the evidence — not the model — decides what gets published.

Method Documents first, narrative second. If it isn't in the record, it isn't in the story.
